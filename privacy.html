PRIVACY POLICY


Here's a simple Privacy Policy template for your portfolio website. Save it as `privacy.html` and link to it from your footer. --- ## Privacy Policy **Last Updated: November 2026**
### 1. Introduction Welcome to my portfolio website. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect any information you provide when you visit my site.
### 2. Information I Collect This is a personal portfolio website. I do **not** collect personal data automatically through cookies, analytics, or tracking scripts.
The only information I may receive is what you voluntarily provide, such as: - Your name and email address (if you contact me via email or a contact form) - Any details you include in your message
### 3. How I Use Your Information If you contact me, I use your information solely to: - Respond to your inquiry - Discuss potential web development or design projects - Follow up on your request I do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties.
### 4. Third-Party Links My website includes links to my social media profiles (LinkedIn, WhatsApp, X/Twitter, Instagram). When you click these links, you leave my site and are subject to the privacy policies of those platforms. I am not responsible for their privacy practices.
### 5. Data Security I take reasonable steps to protect any information you send me. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.
### 6. Your Rights You have the right to: - Request a copy of any personal information I hold about you - Request correction or deletion of your information - Withdraw consent for me to contact you
To exercise these rights, contact me using the details on my Contact page. ### 7. Changes to This Policy I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
### 8. Contact Me If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to your data, please contact me using the information on my Contact page.
--- ### How to use it: 1. Create a new file called `privacy.html` in your project folder 2. Paste the content above inside your HTML structure 3. Update your footer link: ```html Privacy Policy ``` **Note:** This is a general template and not legal advice. If your site collects email addresses through a form, uses analytics (like Google Analytics), or serves ads, you should consult a qualified attorney to ensure your privacy policy complies with applicable laws (such as GDPR, CCPA, or other data protection regulations in your region).
